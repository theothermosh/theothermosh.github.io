Moshiur Rahman

M.S. in Applied Mathematics & Computational Science

North South University, Dhaka, Bangladesh

I am an M.S. candidate in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at North South University, advised by Prof. Dr. Md. Mamun Molla. My research explores the dynamic intersection of high-performance computing, machine learning, and applied mathematics.

Before pursuing my master's degree, I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering with a minor in Mathematics from North South University in 2025. During my undergraduate studies, I developed a strong foundation in research with the Systems and Networks Reading Group under the supervision of Dr. Md. Shahriar Karim. Following graduation, I further honed my expertise as a Research Assistant advised by Dr. Mohammad Shifat-E-Rabbi in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering.
